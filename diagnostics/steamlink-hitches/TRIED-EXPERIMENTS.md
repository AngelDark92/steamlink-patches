## Current direction

**New experiment, headset outcome pending:** [FEC duplicate reservation guard](EXPERIMENT-2026-09-15-fec-duplicate-reservation.md) implements the existing accepted/submitted-ID check before packet-driven decoder allocation even after recovery clears descriptor state. Separate default-off patch for exact 2.0.22/5002322 and 2.0.23/5002363, outside bundles. Use stock UDP plus the current Observe pipeline helper; no decoder staging. See its validation receipt and update this ledger with the matched live outcome before recommending it again. The [proposal](RECOVERY-PATCH-PROPOSAL-2026-09-15.md) also records host 300 ms throttle/retransmit-history amplification, a gated PC reset-priority candidate, and a separate legacy-transport selector. These are not included in the FEC patch, proven fixes, or already-failed experiments.

The UDP trial is unsuccessful and is no longer recommended. Preserve its guarded implementation and evidence for reproducibility; no recommended bundle includes it. Rollback installation remains pending. Investigate transport/FEC frame lifecycle and reset generations: current freezes include repeated old frame work, exhausted decoder input indices, flushes and watchdog recovery despite 0 observed receive-buffer drops. Packet age and the first upstream cause remain unresolved; do not equate the observed regression with proven UDP-induced backlog.

Rationale and version/host boundaries: [Android XR and PC comparison](XR-HOST-COMPARISON-2026-09-15.md). Direct socket-drop and decoder-gap evidence: [Observe results](OBSERVE-RESULTS-2026-09-15.md).
